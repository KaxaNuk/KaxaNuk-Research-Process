## What it says

Catalogues hundreds of published factors and argues that, after adjusting for the multiple testing
implied by the size of the search, a t-statistic near 2.0 is far too weak a bar. A newly proposed
factor needs something closer to 3.0 to be credible.

## What it implies for this strategy

> The standard a new feature should clear before it enters an experiment's hypothesis, and the
> reason **the analyzer's IC table is a screening tool rather than evidence**. An information
> coefficient of a few hundredths is a reason to run a backtest, not a reason to believe a result.
>
> It is also why [`../../RESULTS.md`](../../RESULTS.md) publishes the **trial count** beside every
> winner: a reader cannot discount a best-of-N result without knowing N.
