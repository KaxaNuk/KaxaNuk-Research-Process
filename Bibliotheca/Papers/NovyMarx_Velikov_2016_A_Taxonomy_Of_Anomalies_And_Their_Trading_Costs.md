## What it says

Prices published anomalies net of realistic trading costs and finds many survive far less well than
their gross returns suggest, with the damage concentrated in high-turnover strategies.

## What it implies for this strategy

> The reason results are only ever accepted **net** of the engine's per-share commission on the
> unadjusted price, and the reason turnover is a first-class diagnostic in every experiment notebook
> rather than a footnote.
>
> Record your benchmark's annual turnover here when you have it. Above roughly 200% a year this
> paper says gross results mislead, and the difference between a flat cost model and real per-share
> commission can be a third of a thin edge — which is why this repository has one backtest and it is
> the engine.
